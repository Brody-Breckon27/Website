Basics

Basketball is a world wide sport in which 2 teams with 1-5 players on it they verse each other trying to get the ball in the hoop, there are many different ways to score a basketball, if you score the ball from inside the three point which is a big semi circle around half the court it is a two point shot and if you score behind the three point line the shot is worth three points the goal is to have the most points by the en of the games.I Basketball you are allowed to move with the ball by boncing it up and down once you stop so called "dribbling" the ball you are not allowed to move with the ball. With defending you are not allowed to physically touch a player or a foul will be called so defending from a distance is key. These are pretty much the basics of basketball.
